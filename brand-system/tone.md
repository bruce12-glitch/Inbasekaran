## Do not say
1. “World-class inference research.” — unsupported ranking.
2. “The fastest inference method.” — unsupported comparison.
3. “Guaranteed accurate answers.” — unsupported guarantee.
4. “Revolutionizing AI forever.” — empty superlative.
5. “Trusted by leading labs.” — invented endorsement.
6. “Unlock seamless memory.” — vague marketing language.
7. “Join thousands of collaborators.” — invented count.
8. “Published everywhere.” — false publication implication.
9. “Our cutting-edge solutions.” — generic SaaS language.
10. “I work at a named frontier lab.” — unverified affiliation.

Banned: empower, elevate, seamless, delight, unleash, revolutionary, world-class, best-in-class, and unsupported superlatives.
