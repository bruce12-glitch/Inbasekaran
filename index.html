01

EntroPrefill: Rényi-Guided Context Pruning with Conditional Stability Guarantees for Retrieval-Augmented Generation

Inbasekaran S
arXiv:2610.09757v1 [cs.LG] · Oct 2026 -43.1% TTFT / [arXiv HTML] / [Scholar]

Formulates mid-prefill context pruning as a sink-isolated Order-2 Rényi proposal mechanism coupled to explicit constraints on discarded attention mass, mixture-to-head deletion envelopes, dual optimality-gap certificates, and independent adaptive-layer observer auditing.

Method details ＋
  • Optimized LLM inference efficiency by implementing EntroPrefill, a training-free context pruning method that cut Time-To-First-Token (TTFT) by 43.1%.
  • Engineered an information-theoretic scoring function utilizing Order-2 Rényi entropy on GQA-pooled attention with conditional transformer perturbation bounds.
02

Page-EntroKV: Hardware-Aligned, Entropy-Weighted KV-Cache Eviction under Grouped-Query Attention

Inbasekaran S
arXiv:2610.03135v1 · Oct 2026 UOR 1.000 · 100% Needle Recall / [arXiv HTML] / [Abstract]

A formal framework for KV-cache eviction operating at the hardware tuple (layer, group, page) that GQA serving engines such as vLLM actually allocate, pooling heads via sink-isolated Rényi-2 collision entropy and discrete Hamilton-Hare largest-remainder page allocation.

Method details ＋
  • Formalizes the Union Overhead Ratio (UOR), holding UOR at exactly 1.000 (versus up to 4.75×–5.1× cache inflation under head-independent replay on Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct).
  • Removes a 13× attention-sink masquerade and achieves 100% needle recall at a 20% KV budget where arithmetic-mean pooling collapses to 0%.
03

VendorChain — Zero-Trust B2B Supply-Chain Verification Engine

Inbasekaran S · Undergraduate Research Opportunities Program (UROP) at SRMIST
UROP Research Zero-Trust & Cryptography

End-to-end cryptographic software supply-chain verification architecture combining zero-trust binary intake, automated SBOM attestation, and permissioned blockchain auditability.

Method details ＋
  • Architected a NIST SP 800-207-aligned zero-trust intake pipeline with binary magic-byte validation and AES-256-GCM envelope encryption with in-memory zeroing.
  • Engineered an automated CI/CD supply-chain integrity layer using Syft (CycloneDX SBOM extraction) and Cosign cryptographic signing for tamper-proof artifact provenance.
  • Deployed a private Hyperledger Fabric network with custom Go chaincode persisting immutable SHA-256 artifact hashes, policy verdicts, and timestamps.
